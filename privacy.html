Plain-language policy

Privacy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Your reflection answers

Your choices and written reflections are processed in your browser. They are not sent to Text Him or Not?, Payhip, or Singer Shamrock Enterprises. If you save or print your result, the resulting file remains under your control.

Your access key

When you unlock the full tool, your access key is sent securely to our verification service. The service checks it against Payhip’s license-key system and returns only whether access is valid. We do not intentionally create a separate customer database from that check.

Payments

Purchases and payment details are handled by Payhip and its payment providers. Their privacy terms apply to information you provide during checkout. Text Him or Not? does not receive your full card details.

Browser storage

The tool uses temporary browser storage to preserve your progress and active access during the session. You can remove it by starting over, clearing site data, or closing the session after it expires.

Analytics and advertising

This version does not use advertising trackers or third-party behavioral analytics.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to contact@texthimornot.com.
