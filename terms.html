What the tool provides

Text Him or Not? is a practical reflection and decision-support tool. It uses the information you choose to enter to provide one suggested next step: TEXT HIM, WAIT, or LET GO.

No guaranteed outcome

The tool cannot know another person’s thoughts, predict a response, or guarantee contact, reconciliation, attraction, or a relationship. Its result is guidance, not a promise.

Not professional advice

The tool is not therapy, medical care, legal advice, crisis support, or a substitute for professional help. If contact could put you or someone else at risk, do not rely on this tool; prioritize safety and contact appropriate local support.

Boundaries come first

You agree not to use the tool to bypass a request for space or no contact, to harass, monitor, pressure, deceive, or manipulate another person. A message template is never permission to cross a boundary.
